One final suggestion: treat this as a **governing document**, not a coding prompt. Use it at the start of major planning sessions or architectural reviews. For day-to-day implementation, create much smaller prompts focused on one capability (for example, "design the asset model" or "improve onboarding"). Large strategic prompts are excellent for setting direction, but smaller execution prompts tend to produce more focused, actionable results.
